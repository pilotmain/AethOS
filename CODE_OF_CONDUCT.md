# Code of conduct

## Our standard

AethOS contributors, maintainers, and community members must make participation
welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment. Be constructive, assume good
intent while addressing impact, respect different experience levels, and keep
technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data,
or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation, contact project lead `@pilotmain` through the contact
method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If that person is involved, use
GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel. Do not publish sensitive evidence.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence to the extent possible and may
issue a correction, warning, temporary restriction, permanent ban, or other
proportionate response. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports are also a
violation. Decisions may be appealed once with new information.
